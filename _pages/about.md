I'm an Associate Professor of Technology Management and Innovation at NYU Tandon, with an associated appointment at NYU Stern. I lead the **DECODE Lab** (Data-Enabled Computation, Optimization and Decision Engineering), where we study how organizations make better decisions under uncertainty.

My work spans pricing, assortment, inventory, and fulfillment, with a focus on settings where data is limited or uncertain. I also apply these tools to problems with social and environmental impact, from disaster relief and food waste to seed conservation in genebanks. I draw on optimization, algorithm design, and distributionally robust methods.

I'm always looking for PhD students interested in rigorous theory with practical impact, so please reach out!
